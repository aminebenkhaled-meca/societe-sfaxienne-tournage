YOUR QUESTIONS

Before you entrust us with a part.

Contact
Can you reproduce a worn part?

Yes, after inspection and dimensional measurement. A heavily worn part may require measurements of mating components to establish the functional dimensions.

Can a pump be repaired instead of replaced?

It depends on the condition of the casing, shaft, impeller and working surfaces. Diagnosis helps compare repair and replacement options.

Can you work directly on a boat?

An on-site intervention can be assessed according to access, the work required and availability. Some operations require dismantling and returning the part to the workshop.

What should I send for a repair enquiry?

Send photographs, the equipment reference, symptoms, known dimensions and location. Photos can be sent directly in the WhatsApp conversation.

Can you work from a drawing?

Yes. We check the material, functional dimensions, fits and feasibility with you before agreeing the manufacturing work.

Do you work outside Sfax?

We assess requests within approximately 150 km of Sfax and farther away depending on the scale of the work. There is one workshop, in Sfax.

How do I request urgent assistance?

Call Yassin Ben Khaled or Tarek Kallel and describe the breakdown and your location. Urgent intervention is assessed subject to availability; there is no round-the-clock service.
