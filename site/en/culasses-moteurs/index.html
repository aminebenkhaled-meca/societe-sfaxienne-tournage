03

A methodical approach

Inspection of the part and relevant surfaces comes before any proposal. We define which work is feasible and which operations may require an external specialist.

Checks and limitations

Specific resurfacing, pressure testing or valve-seat machining is not advertised as a standard capability. Feasibility and checks are agreed after inspection.

Preparing your enquiry

Provide the engine model, references and symptoms. Bring the dismantled part where possible, along with the fault history and details of previous repairs.
