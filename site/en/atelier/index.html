A local workshop. A repair-first mindset.

Société Sfaxienne de Tournage supports marine professionals and mechanical maintenance needs in Sfax, with 8 years of hands-on experience.

08years of experience

A team of four

We value direct discussion to understand operating conditions, inspect the part and agree the work. Repair where suitable, reproduce when a part is unavailable and check before handover: this approach guides our work.

Two conventional lathes

The workshop has two lathes with stated dimensions of 1 m and 2 m. Geometry, workholding and usable capacity are checked for every enquiry. A detailed technical sheet will be added once the specifications are approved.

The right resources for each operation

Some operations may involve technical partners. Heat treatment is outsourced. Specialist checks are offered only when suitable equipment and expertise are available.

Terms agreed together

Our work may be covered by a warranty defined according to the nature of the work and the agreed terms.
